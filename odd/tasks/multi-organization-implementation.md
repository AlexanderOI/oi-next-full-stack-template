## Confirmed product decisions

- Platform administrator assignments use a dedicated platform-assignment table; organization role assignments are membership-scoped in a distinct table. Never fall back to ambiguous global `user_role` semantics.

- Global identities may belong to multiple organizations; email and username are globally unique.
- Public self-registration creates a username-derived personal organization and owner membership.
- Admin provisioning starts only in the active organization, without a personal organization; creator may select unrestricted or permanently restricted mode using the protected permission.
- Restriction cannot be removed. Allowlist exceptions are permanently non-revocable; no self-grant or recipient-org self-authorization. Frozen orphan allowlists cannot expand.
- Invitation creator selects the membership role applied upon acceptance; acceptance revalidates policy and preserves active organization. Inviting protected `owner` additionally requires `organization.ownership.transfer`; admins cannot invite owners. Invitation expires after 2 days; no pending invitation cap.
- Unknown email addresses may be invited; recipients sign up with the same normalized email, verify, and then accept. Public signup still creates their personal organization; acceptance never switches active context.
- Explicit re-invite/resend replaces a pending invitation with a fresh canonical two-day invitation, invalidating the old token; a partial unique index allows one pending invitation per normalized org/email, and concurrent uniqueness conflicts return existing state.
- Canonical `invitation.role` stores a product role ID; acceptance resolves it and writes `membershipRole`, while provider `member.role` stays the safe lifecycle value `member`.
- Maximum 100 organizations per user and 1,000 active members per organization.
- Protected role definitions are global; custom roles and role assignments are organization/membership-scoped.
- Organization role grants: owner receives all organization permissions; admin receives ordinary organization administration except organization deletion, ownership transfer, restriction creation, and allowlist-grant read/create; member receives only organization and member read. No organization role inherits platform permissions.
- Suspending the sole active membership suspends the identity globally; with multiple active memberships only the selected membership is suspended.
- Removing/leaving the final membership outside org deletion atomically deletes the identity and sessions. Organization deletion also removes identities with only that membership; restricted users with surviving allowed memberships continue under frozen allowlist.
- Session active organization and URL organization must match and have an active membership.
- Resettable database; no backfill.

- Self-signup and admin provisioning follow their distinct identity lifecycles.
- Invitations select a valid org role, expire in two days, accept atomically, and cannot bypass restriction/allowlist rules.
- Protected/custom roles, membership suspension/removal, last-owner continuity, and deletion/orphan behavior follow confirmed policy.
- Relevant focused tests, type-check, lint, migration generation/validation, and production build pass as applicable.
- No unrelated worktree changes are overwritten or included.
